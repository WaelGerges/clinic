# Arabic/English, smarter scheduling and visit documents

Implemented 12 September 2026 in the shared Clinic Assistant / Clinic Doctor package.

## Language

Choose English or العربية before signing in, or from More. The choice is stored securely on the device. Arabic uses right-to-left navigation and localized Material date/time controls. Interface labels are translated; patient names, clinical notes, diagnoses and medication instructions remain as entered. Dates and scheduling continue to use Africa/Cairo.

## Scheduling

Appointments retain a duration (30 minutes for existing records). The form offers duration choices and 15-minute slot suggestions accounting for the duration, working periods, breaks and existing active bookings. Suggestions require shared settings to load successfully; offline saves remain local until synchronization is confirmed.

Doctor administrators configure recurring weekly working periods in More → Working hours and waitlist. Split a day into two periods to create a break. After configuration, days without periods are closed. Existing bookings are retained when working hours change. Before any configuration, 09:00–17:00 is used only for suggestions, not as enforced clinic hours.

The cancellation waitlist stores a patient once per doctor. Staff can add patients from a new appointment form, book from the waitlist, and remove an entry after confirming the booking. It does not send automatic messages.

The new server migration adds duration storage, clinic-scoped scheduling settings and waitlist RPCs, private tables with RLS, audit events and overlap/working-period validation. Existing operation receipts, appointment identities and authorization checks are preserved. A clinic transaction lock serializes booking writes. Cancelled, no-show and completed appointments do not block a new slot.

## Printed documents

Open a patient → Visits or Prescriptions → expand a completed visit → Print visit documents. Choose a prescription or visit summary; doctors can also prepare a referral letter. The document language can be selected independently of the app language. An embedded, OFL-licensed Amiri font supports Arabic without downloading fonts at print time.

The native print/share preview produces clinic-branded PDFs with patient identity, visit date, revision and page numbers. Prescriptions exclude general clinical notes. All clinical text is printed as recorded, rather than translated or generated. A signature line is provided; documents are not digitally signed. Connected workspaces refresh the exact completed visit before printing, including visits beyond the first history page. Clinical-read permission and matching clinic/patient identity are required. Referral letters require the doctor role.
